# Privacy Policy for FluxRush Mobile

**Last Updated:** June 28, 2026

At **FluxRush**, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle device camera permissions and local data when you play **FluxRush Mobile**.

---

## 1. Camera Access & Usage
- **Purpose**: The app requests permission to access your front-facing device camera. This permission is used exclusively to capture frames in real-time to detect hand landmarks (specifically your index finger, thumb, and knuckles) for gesture-based game controls.
- **On-Device Local Processing**: All camera processing is performed entirely **on your device** in real-time. Camera frames are processed in memory and are **never** saved, recorded, or written to disk.
- **No Transmission**: None of the video streams, frames, images, or tracking coordinates are ever transmitted over the network or shared with any external servers.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Storage
- **Personal Data**: The app does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).
- **Local Settings & High Scores**: Your high score and game preferences (e.g. quality settings, control modes) are saved locally on your device's memory. This data is not uploaded anywhere and is deleted if you uninstall the app.
- **Third-Party Services**: We do not use third-party analytics libraries, advertising trackers, or telemetry tools.
